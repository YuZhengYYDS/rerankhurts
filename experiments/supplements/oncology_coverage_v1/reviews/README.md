# Oncology support review workbook

[Download the review workbook](oncology_review_ai_assisted.xlsx). It contains 377 fact-level entries and the associated source evidence. The original workbook is preserved unchanged, including its labels describing AI assistance.

The authors report that an oncology expert subsequently reviewed and confirmed all 377 judgments. The expert's name and institution are withheld for privacy, as requested by the authors. The workbook itself identifies ChatGPT as the AI-assisted reviewer and explicitly states that it is not evidence of an independently completed or signed human review. It does not include an individual human signature or an independent human attestation. The authors' report of later expert review is therefore distinct from what can be verified from this workbook alone.

This is not a clinical gold-standard dataset, and uploading this workbook does not change the frozen annotations, their adjudication metadata, or the reported results. The 60-world human audit is a separate review and is not documented by this workbook. For original annotations, evidence, and scoring context, see the [coverage package](../README.md) and [artifact guide](../../../../ARTIFACTS.md).
